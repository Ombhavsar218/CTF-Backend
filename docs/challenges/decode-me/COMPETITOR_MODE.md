# Decode Me — Competitor Mode Content

**Stage:** 2 — Content preparation  
**Audience:** Competitor Mode players.

---

## Scenario

A field note arrived as a single line of ordinary-looking characters. It contains no spaces, no service address, and no obvious words. Someone said it was “only a representation,” but did not say which one.

Your objective is to determine what happened to the note, reverse it, and use the recovered clue to identify the flag.

---

## Challenge objective

Recover the flag from `decode-me-message.txt` using local tools and reasoning only.

---

## Artifact download

1. Open the Decode Me challenge page.
2. Go to the **Files** tab.
3. Download `decode-me-message.txt`.
4. Inspect and decode it locally.

There is no Docker instance and no connection information for this challenge.

---

## Flag submission

1. Use the decoded clue to determine the exact flag.
2. Enter it in the challenge's flag submission field.
3. Submit.

The flag uses the platform's `FLAG{...}` format. Preserve exact capitalization and separators. Correct submissions are recorded once per account.

---

## Rules

1. Solve locally; no external website is required.
2. Do not share the flag or solving details during an active event.
3. Do not modify or replace the artifact.
4. Submit the complete flag, not a fragment or description.
5. Incorrect attempts may be recorded; repeated correct submissions do not earn extra points.

---

## Suggested time limit

5–10 minutes for a player familiar with common encodings. Beginners may take longer; the challenge is intended to be approachable rather than timed.
